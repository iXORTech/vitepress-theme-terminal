# Deep Dive

A leaf page three folders deep (`demo/advanced/advanced-2/deep-dive`) — the
deepest node of the explorer demo tree.

Worth noticing here:

- the explorer indents each level along a guide line, editor-tree style;
- this row carries the accent highlight while the page is open;
- the `advanced` folder above shows its **open** folder glyph now, and
  remembers being open if you reload.
- the `advanced-2` folder has no Markdown index; its localized label comes from
  the adjacent `explorer.json`, while its collapsed default follows its depth.

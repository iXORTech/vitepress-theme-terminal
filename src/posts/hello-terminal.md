# Hello, Terminal

Welcome to the first demo post of **VitePress Theme Terminal**. This page lives
under `src/posts/`, so the theme renders it with the *post* page type — a byline
with its categories and tags, followed by the article body and the end-of-article
license and comment cards.

## Declaring taxonomy

A post declares its `categories` and `tags` in frontmatter. They become links to
the category and tag listing pages, and the post shows up under each of them in
the archives.
